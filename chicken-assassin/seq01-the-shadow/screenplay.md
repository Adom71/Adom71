# CHICKEN ASSASSIN
## Sequence 1 — "The Shadow"

*Draft 1 · Opening of the film · Estimated length: 3–4 minutes*

**Purpose:** introduce Monasville, the Syndicate, Alexander Voss and FLYCORP. Show why the mayor is
a problem for them. Send the B-ros to kill him, and have them meet a figure standing in the road.
We never see his face. The audience shouldn't know yet that it's Mason.

---

**FADE IN:**

**EXT. MONASVILLE — SKYLINE — NIGHT**

Silence. Then the low hum of a city.

Monasville at night. Orange streetlights. Thousands of lit office windows. Above them all
stands **FLYCORP TOWER**: the tallest building in the city, its crown glowing red, the blue
**FLYCORP** sign burning near the top.

> **NEWS ANCHOR (V.O.)**
> (on a car radio, warm and routine)
> ...and Mayor Robert Mitchell spoke again tonight at the Monasville Charity Gala, repeating
> his promise to root out corruption in the city...

**INT. FLYCORP TOWER — TOP FLOOR — VOSS'S OFFICE — NIGHT**

Dark glass, polished wood, the city glittering below. A wall screen plays the news with no sound.

On screen: **MAYOR ROBERT MITCHELL** (50s, bison, honest face, tired eyes) at a podium.

**ALEXANDER VOSS** (50s, bull, perfect suit, calm as still water) stands at the window with his
back to us. A gold ring catches the light. On it is the mark of **THE SYNDICATE**: a red,
burning rooster.

He touches a remote. The mayor's voice fills the room.

> **MAYOR MITCHELL (ON SCREEN)**
> Whoever is poisoning this city, hiding behind clean buildings and clean suits... I will find
> you. And I will show Monasville who you are.

Voss mutes him. A long beat.

> **VOSS**
> (quietly, to no one)
> Brave man.

He picks up a phone. He doesn't raise his voice.

> **VOSS (CONT'D)**
> Send the B-ros. Tonight.
> (beat)
> Make it quiet.

He hangs up and turns back to the window. In the glass, his reflection sits right over the
mayor's face on the screen.

**INT. B-ROS' CAR — MOVING — NIGHT**

A black sedan. Rap music thumping low. Dashboard glow.

**BILLY** (20s, driving, loud, cocky, chewing gum) taps the wheel to the beat. Next to him,
**BILL** (20s, quiet, cold) loads a pistol round by round. A small red light blinks on the
**dashcam** between them.

A phone buzzes. Bill reads it.

> **BILL**
> Gala's over. He's taking Ridge Road home. Two cars. No police.

> **BILLY**
> (grinning)
> Ridge Road. Dark, long, no cameras. It's like he wants it.

> **BILL**
> Boss said quiet.

> **BILLY**
> I'm always quiet.

He turns the music up. Bill turns it off.

**EXT. RIDGE ROAD — NIGHT**

The edge of the city. A long two-lane road through dark pine trees. Streetlights every forty
metres, each one a small island of orange light in the fog. Far away, the skyline glows.

The **MAYOR'S SUV** drives past, steady and calm.

**INT. MAYOR'S SUV — MOVING — NIGHT**

Mayor Mitchell sits in the back, reading glasses on, a thick folder open on his knees.
Photographs. Bank records. A list of names. One page is stamped **FLYCORP**.

His **DRIVER, SAM** (40s) glances at him in the mirror.

> **SAM**
> You should rest, sir.

> **MAYOR MITCHELL**
> (not looking up)
> When this is finished, Sam.

He closes the folder and rubs his eyes. He looks out of the window...

**EXT. RIDGE ROAD — ROADSIDE — CONTINUOUS**

...and as the SUV passes one streetlight, we see **a FIGURE** standing just outside its
light, among the trees. Hooded. Still. Watching.

**INT. MAYOR'S SUV — CONTINUOUS**

The mayor turns to look back. Nothing there now, just the dark and the trees.

He frowns, then lets it go. The SUV drives on.

**EXT. RIDGE ROAD — FURTHER BACK — NIGHT**

The B-ros' car comes up the road **with its headlights off**, closing the gap.

**INT. B-ROS' CAR — MOVING — NIGHT**

The mayor's tail lights shine red far ahead.

> **BILLY**
> After the bend. I pull alongside, you do your thing.

Bill raises the pistol and screws on a silencer.

They round the bend. Billy flicks the **headlights ON**...

**EXT. RIDGE ROAD — THE BEND — CONTINUOUS**

...and the beams land on **THE FIGURE**, standing in the **middle of the road**.

Black hooded coat. Mask. The hilt of a sword over one shoulder. A small flash of **red** where
a comb shows under the hood. He doesn't move.

**INT. B-ROS' CAR — CONTINUOUS**

> **BILLY**
> WHOA—!

He **slams the brakes**.

**EXT. RIDGE ROAD — CONTINUOUS**

Tyres scream on the wet road. The car slides and stops about **forty metres** from the
figure. Steam rises from the hood.

Far ahead, the mayor's tail lights shrink into the dark, then **disappear**. He never knew.

Silence. The engine idles. A streetlight above the figure **buzzes and flickers**.

**INT. B-ROS' CAR — CONTINUOUS**

> **BILLY**
> (half laughing)
> Is this guy serious?

> **BILL**
> (staring)
> ...Go around him.

> **BILLY**
> Go around him? He's in the road. In the road is the road.

Billy throws his door open.

**EXT. RIDGE ROAD — CONTINUOUS**

Billy and Bill step out into the headlights. Their long shadows stretch toward the figure.

> **BILLY**
> (calling out)
> Hey! Old man! You lost?

The figure says nothing.

> **BILLY (CONT'D)**
> You got about three seconds to get out of my road.

A beat. When the figure finally speaks, his voice is low and calm, almost kind.

> **THE FIGURE**
> Turn around.
> (beat)
> Go home.

Billy laughs. Bill doesn't. He lifts the silenced pistol slowly.

> **BILLY**
> We are going home. Right after—

The figure's hand **moves**. So fast we almost miss it.

**SHHK.** A **shuriken** spins through the headlight beam...

**...and one headlight EXPLODES.** Glass rains down. Half the light is gone.

Bill **fires**. **PFFT. PFFT.** Sparks off the road. The figure is no longer there.

> **BILLY**
> Where'd he—

The flickering streetlight goes **OUT**.

**DARKNESS.**

**EXT. RIDGE ROAD — THE FIGHT — CONTINUOUS**

We see the fight only in **flashes**: the last headlight, muzzle flashes, sparks.

- **FLASH:** Bill spins, firing at shadows.
- **FLASH:** the figure, right behind him.
- **FLASH:** a knife knocks the pistol from Bill's hand.
- **FLASH:** Billy charges with a blade, shouting.
- **SOUND:** a sword sliding out of its sheath. Slow. Clean.
- **FLASH:** the steel catches the light...

Silence.

The streetlight **buzzes back on**.

Bill stands frozen in the road, eyes wide. The figure stands behind him, perfectly still,
sword lowered.

> **BILL**
> (a whisper)
> ...It's you.

**SLASH.**

**BLOOD SPLASHES ACROSS THE SCREEN.**

**CUT TO BLACK.**

A heartbeat of silence. Then a deep, heavy drum hit, and:

# CHICKEN ASSASSIN

**BEAT. IN THE BLACK:**

The faint, steady **beep... beep...** of a machine.

**INT. B-ROS' CAR — LATER — NIGHT**

Empty. Doors open. Rain begins to fall on the windshield.

On the dashboard, the small **red light of the dashcam** keeps blinking.

**Still recording.**

Close on a **shuriken** stuck deep in the car door. Engraved on it is a faded mark: an old,
scratched-out version of the **Syndicate's burning rooster**.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

*END OF SEQUENCE 1*

## Notes for the team

**What this sets up for later sequences:**

- **The mayor's folder (FLYCORP page):** the evidence he keeps collecting in Sequence 2.
- **The dashcam:** the Syndicate later finds the footage. That plus the old Syndicate mark on the
  shuriken is how Voss learns one of his former assassins is alive. That leads to the attack on
  the Reed family in Sequences 4–5.
- **Bill's "It's you":** Bill recognises the fighting style from Syndicate legend, a hint
  that the figure is a famous former assassin. It also pays off later when Voss recognises the
  same style in Marcus.
- **"Turn around. Go home.":** Mason gives them a chance first. He protects, he doesn't hunt.
  That's who he is now.
- **Red comb under the hood:** the only clue to who he is. After Sequence 3 the audience may
  connect it to Mason.

**Why the fight is shown in flashes:** it's cheaper and easier to animate than a fully lit fight,
it's scarier, and it keeps Mason a mystery. Each flash is one short AI video shot.

**Questions for you to decide:**

1. **Do both B-ros die here,** or does one survive? (In this draft both die.)
2. **What animal are Billy and Bill?** Their character sheet isn't fully clear. Roosters?
3. **Is the Syndicate mark a "burning rooster"?** That's my reading of the red emblem on your
   cast lineup. Change it if your friend had something else in mind.
4. **The music:** the B-ros play rap in the car, and Mason and Marcus listen to rap together
   in Sequence 3. Is that a deliberate echo, or should the B-ros' music be different?
